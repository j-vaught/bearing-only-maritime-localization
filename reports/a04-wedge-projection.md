# A04 full-map wedge projection audit

The frozen full-map wrapper constructs its `LocalFrame` from the midpoint of the EPSG:32618 map bounds. For the port-001 trial, that origin is (-73.8800603364, 35.1821273568), and the default centered azimuthal-equidistant frame receives all 66,668 catalog positions and all five bearings. The audited trial is `development/port-001/placement-001/r0/range10000m/typesoff/nominal`. Its maximum range is 10,000 m and the configured grid-bearing discrepancy is 0.5 degrees.

The source-site grid-north discrepancy spans -5.232286834 to +5.175548905 degrees across the catalog. Its maximum absolute discrepancy is 5.232286834 degrees, the nearest-rank 95th percentile is 4.036489716 degrees, and 59,414 of 66,668 sources (89.1192 percent) exceed the claimed 0.5-degree bound. The maximum occurs at catalog key `source:96` at (-81.663580, 45.258060), about 1,298.8 km from the frame origin. The 95th-percentile source is `enc-component:approach:Approach.Daymark_point:US4FL2MD.000:source_object_id=2268618` at (-81.628050, 29.3777085).

For the 333,340 reverse-ray endpoints formed by pairing every catalog source with each frozen port-001 bearing and extending 10 km along the reversed geodesic, the true bearing from each endpoint back to its catalog source differs from the same chord's frame-grid bearing by -5.230401477 to +5.147129306 degrees. The maximum absolute discrepancy is 5.230401477 degrees, the nearest-rank 95th percentile is 4.050880850 degrees, and 296,737 endpoints (89.0193 percent) exceed 0.5 degrees. The maximum event uses measurement bearing 194.027981558 degrees at `source:96`; its reverse ray starts at 14.027981558 degrees and ends at (-81.632651041, 45.345350919). At that endpoint, the true bearing back to the source is 194.049966501 degrees while the projected-grid bearing is 199.280367978 degrees. Across the five measurements, per-bearing maxima range from 5.135974515 to 5.230401477 degrees and nearest-rank 95th percentiles range from 4.031787458 to 4.095797918 degrees.

The 0.5-degree discrepancy bound is not defensible for this full-coast frame. The observed maximum is 10.46 times the claimed bound at catalog sites and 10.46 times the bound at 10 km ray endpoints. Since the wrapper adds this scalar to its angular tolerance, the wedge raster may under-cover true-north reverse-ray directions; proposal generation remains heuristic and cannot certify exclusion. The separate frozen search output remains `budget_exhausted` and `ambiguous`; this audit does not alter it. The audit reads the frozen measurements and catalog only. It does not use observer truth, alter frozen inputs, rerun search, or make a coverage claim beyond the measured finite set.

The smallest correction is to use bounded geographic tiles with a frame and a separately qualified convergence bound for each tile, checking catalog sources and the full requested ray range before applying a scalar widening term. A more precise interface accepts a per-source convergence or projected grid-bearing correction, with grid bearing computed as true bearing minus the measured true-minus-grid convergence, and a separately measured finite-distance residual. If the residual is material, construct the proposal ray from WGS84 geodesic samples projected into the tile rather than assuming one grid direction. Neither interface should let proposals exclude candidates from the combined objective.

The audit script is `tools/audit_a04_wedge_projection.py`. It ran on the server against the frozen A26 port-001 inputs in 4.80 seconds with 753,712 KiB maximum resident memory. Verification used Python 3.11.15. Ruff and ty passed, the focused accuracy suite passed with three tests, and `git diff --check` passed. The calculation output is retained under the server's ignored `.agent-work/outputs/a04-wedge-projection-audit.json` for reproducibility.


## Reverse-bearing geodesic primitive

Added `maritime_localization_shared.geospatial.reverse_bearing.solve_reverse_bearing_observer(catalog_lonlat_deg, desired_bearing_deg, range_m, *, tolerance_deg=1e-9, max_iterations=12) -> ReverseBearingResult`. The catalog position is the geodesic endpoint, and the requested true-north initial bearing is measured from the unknown observer toward that catalog point. Inputs broadcast over their leading dimensions. Results contain read-only observer longitude/latitude, signed independently checked inverse-bearing residuals, convergence flags, and explicit zero-range degeneracy flags. The solver starts from the reversed bearing at the catalog point, corrects its forward azimuth using the endpoint back-azimuth residual, and verifies the final bearing independently with `Geod.inv`. The [PyProj Geod documentation](https://pyproj4.github.io/pyproj/3.7.2/api/geod.html) defines the forward method's terminus back-azimuth output used by this correction. It returns a finite last position and false convergence flag on failure rather than substituting a guessed point. Zero range returns the catalog position, a zero residual placeholder, `degenerate=True`, and `converged=False` because its bearing is undefined.

The 14 focused tests pass. The East-Coast validation covered four positions from Florida to Maine, eight cardinal/intercardinal desired bearings, and ranges 0.1 m, 1 m, 100 m, 1 km, and 10 km. Every 100 m to 10 km case converged within 1e-9 degrees, and the largest independently measured range error across the sweep was 1.68e-9 m. At 0.1 m and 1 m, longitude/latitude representation limits leave some independent inverse residuals above the strict 1e-9-degree tolerance. Eight of 32 cases converged at each of these two short ranges; the largest residuals were 4.49e-7 degrees and 4.64e-8 degrees, respectively, and each returned status reflects that residual. Separate date-line and near-pole cases converged, zero-range behavior was verified, and a 20,000 km case returned finite coordinates with explicit nonconvergence.

Python 3.11.15 checks passed. Ruff and ty passed, `pytest -q tests/accuracy/test_a04_reverse_bearing.py` passed with 14 tests, and `git diff --check` passed. The solver is a geometry primitive only. It does not alter the wedge generator or certify any candidate exclusion.
