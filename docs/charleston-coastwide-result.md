# Corrected Charleston search across the Eastern Seaboard

The completed coast-wide search selected a position 2.060304 meters from the simulated Charleston observer. The estimate is latitude 32.77001838830661, longitude -79.90500313407134. Truth is latitude 32.77, longitude -79.905. Error is the WGS84 ellipsoidal distance without alignment, fitted correction or truth-based refinement. The selected candidate matches all 64 positive chart-aid reports with consistent status and zero numerical flags.

The old run was stopped at the user's request, with its outputs preserved. Before launching its replacement, regional and coast starting-grid profiles identified region pruning and visible-object prediction as the dominant costs. The regional profiled outputs were numerically identical to the prior regional test. The [preflight analysis](resident-search-profiling.md) and its raw records were saved before the full launch.

The replacement used GPUs0, 1 and 2 and the original full Eastern Seaboard rectangle. It completed all 39,435 initial 10-kilometer roots, refining every survivor to terminal widths no greater than 1 meter. No top-N cutoff, true-position seed or Charleston-only search prior was used. The solver received the full 34,436-object canonical catalog, the committed corrected observations and the saved map/index. The exact input frame is the same one used in the earlier regional check.

The search evaluated 1,337,411 regions, pruned 976,768 and scored 36,149 terminal centers in 98.495 seconds of search-loop wall time. Of the terminal centers, 148 were consistent, 20,379 inconsistent, 15,441 unresolved and 181 land centers. Every consistent center has a full injective 64-marker match. The fixed selector minimized summed absolute bearing residual among consistent candidates with zero flags, breaking ties by east and north coordinates. Selection used GPU0 after the search completed.

The estimate was written and hashed before the separate evaluator read truth. Independent review reproduced the exact selected point and residual from all 465 saved leaf archives, verified the settings, selector, manifest, summary, estimate and combined leaf-output hashes, and independently recomputed the 2.060304-meter error. The initial automatic evaluator inherited an old regional wording label; that label was generalized and the same immutable estimate re-evaluated. Neither the estimate nor its numerical error changed.

The full run saved every tenth whole batch and its nested timing records. It sampled 985 of 9,845 batches. Approximately 72.9 percent of measured sampled device-stream spans were region pruning, 25.7 percent visible-object prediction and 0.2 percent cooperative matching. The main custom kernels were prune_chart_geometry and pp_pairs. These proportions describe the sampled instrumented spans, not complete wall-time accounting, pure kernel busy time or occupancy. Parent/child spans overlap, and device times overlap across GPUs. CPU call profiles from preflight, host call durations, result-write timings, process CPU/I/O counters, GPU readings and per-batch progress were saved for analysis.

Both the original base cache and canonical object cache were hits. Rebuilding was blocked. The base archive retains its SHA-256 digest, size and nanosecond modification time exactly. GPU3 was excluded from the full search. This test covers one corrected simulated observation frame over the complete declared coast domain; unresolved centers remain unresolved, and association uniqueness and real-vessel accuracy were not evaluated.

The [complete result, protocol and profile summaries](verification/charleston-coastwide-result.json), [all terminal centers with their manifest](verification/charleston-coastwide-terminal-results.npz), and [raw full-run profiles and health records](verification/charleston-coastwide-profiles.zip) are saved together. The input measurements are the previously committed [corrected frame](verification/charleston-blind-measurements.json). The NPZ contains numeric arrays and manifest_utf8 JSON bytes and requires no pickle. Further performance work should start with the pruning and prediction geometry kernels identified by these measurements.
