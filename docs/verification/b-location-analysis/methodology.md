**Detected chart-aid congregation analysis.**

J.C. Vaught. October 29, 2026.

The saved final B1–B24 suite supplies 24 observer sites and 933 detected physical-aid reports. Each report was joined by its saved observation index to a detected simulation-diagnostic group, then by that group's representative catalog key to the canonical nominal simulation catalog. All 24 detected-aid counts match the final suite. Therefore the primary metrics describe the physical origins of the reports used by the search. Vessel reports, clutter, missed groups, and aids merely located within the visibility radius do not enter these metrics.

The simulator resolves a group of angularly inseparable physical objects by choosing the nearest object, with its object ID breaking equal-range ties. That object's position and noiseless bearing define the report origin. Consequently the primary data contains one point per detected physical-aid report. All saved angular-group member IDs remain attached to each report, and all joined member coordinates remain available in aid-clustering-evidence.json. The separate nearcoincident-member diagnostic includes physical-aid members of these detected physical-aid groups, including members that did not supply the representative report. It does not count members of groups reported as vessels.

The catalog was canonicalized with the existing exact-coordinate identity routine. This operation merges eligible chart components only when their parsed latitude and longitude values are identical. No coordinate rounding or distance-based identity merge was introduced. The catalog contains 66,668 raw records and 34,436 canonical records across all object types. Its SHA-256 is cab16ce1d5b82863cd0654e564e84bf23cda4f720918eddad9ffcbedda65a2f0, matching both saved suite manifests. The hashes of the identity, simulator runner, and angular grouping sources also match both manifests. Original suite commit ab98948828a767cabc6c6e8a3d969b78f3ce1709 and retry suite commit e948673293bdab70f447896bbd2a66a125cf1444 identify those saved runs.

Geographic distances use the World Geodetic System 1984 ellipsoid through pyproj.Geod. Visible-feature east and north offsets from the canonical record are applied before measuring distance, matching the saved nominal simulator's target construction. The suite supplied no displacement mismatch. The median nearest-neighbor distance is the median, over report origins, of the smallest geodesic distance to a different report origin. Self-distances are excluded. Median pairwise distance uses all unordered pairs. Median observer range and the 90th percentile observer range measure distance from the saved observer, with linear interpolation for the percentile. The latter describes range coverage around the observer and is not a radius around the aid centroid.

The point export also provides local east and north coordinates. These form an observer-centered azimuthal equidistant representation, with $e=r\sin\theta$ and $n=r\cos\theta$, where $r$ is ellipsoidal geodesic range and $\theta$ is the initial true-north azimuth. Both coordinates are in meters. The saved observer is at the origin. This representation gives comparable local scatterplots without using the search-map projection. Geographic nearest-neighbor and pairwise metrics continue to use ellipsoidal distances between points.

Angular congregation is reported twice. Observed metrics use the saved noisy measurement bearings. Noiseless metrics use bearings reconstructed from the saved observer and joined representative physical origins. The occupied angular span is $360^\circ$ minus the largest circular gap between sorted bearings. The circular nearest-neighbor separation for each report is the smaller gap to its two adjacent bearings, and the reported metric is the median of those separations. Coincident bearings retain zero separation. The largest absolute observed-minus-noiseless wrapped residual is within the saved $0.5^\circ$ bound at every site.

The optional bearing-axis eigenvalue ratio summarizes directional coverage. It is the smaller eigenvalue divided by the larger eigenvalue of the mean outer product of transverse unit bearing directions. Its value ranges from zero for a common bearing axis to one for balanced perpendicular axes. It ignores target range, measurement noise, association ambiguity, and map constraints. It is a descriptive angular anisotropy statistic and does not estimate localization uncertainty or an information bound.

Runtime and pruning fields are copied from the final saved suite. Search time is the saved search_s field in seconds. Skipped sites have no search time. The pruned fraction is the saved pruned count divided by evaluated count. No search, simulation, scoring, cache build, or benchmark was executed for this analysis. These descriptive comparisons do not establish that geographic congregation or angular concentration caused a failure or runtime difference.

**Nearcoincident source members.**

All 24 sites have zero representative-origin pairs separated by less than one meter. Thus the primary nearest-neighbor distances do not collapse toward zero because of the nearcoincident catalog records.

The saved detected physical-aid groups nevertheless contain 159 site-level pairs of distinct canonical aid members separated by less than one centimeter. These occur at B2, B6, B7, B18, B19, B20, B21, B22, and B23. The first eight sites returned no estimate; B23 was skipped for having only 15 detected aid reports. Minimum member separations at those sites range from approximately $5.6\times10^{-9}$ to $1.1\times10^{-7}$ meters. Their IDs and full-precision coordinates are retained in aid-clustering-nearcoincident.json. These are nominal-coordinate distinctions preserved by the exact-coordinate identity rule. The descriptive join does not localize a search flag or prove a failure mechanism.

**Reproduction and evidence.**

The main JSON and site-level CSV are aid-clustering.json and aid-clustering.csv. Report-origin coordinates are also exported to aid-clustering-points.csv. The evidence JSON contains the saved measurements, diagnostics, observer coordinates, settings, source hashes, catalog hash, and selected canonical catalog records for every site. Skipped attempts have no saved settings file, so their original suite manifest supplies the shared settings and is named explicitly as the settings source.

Run uv sync in this directory, then uv run python extract_aid_clustering.py to reproduce every metric from the local evidence. Add --refresh-evidence only to reread the existing saved remote files and catalog. The local evidence is sufficient for offline metric reproduction. Ruff formatting, Ruff checking, and ty checking passed for the extraction script. Small hand-case checks cover circular wraparound, coincident bearings, balanced and collinear bearing axes, quantile interpolation, and a known geodesic distance. Data checks verify all site counts, observer coordinates, unique representative IDs, unique exact representative positions, and every bearing residual against the saved bound.
