# Primary source checks

Betke and Gurvits, 1997. Author record confirms identified-landmark bearing localization and bibliographic details. DOI 10.1109/70.563647. https://www.cs.bu.edu/faculty/betke/research/publications.html.

Colle and Galerne, 2013. Publisher abstract confirms generic goniometric measurements, bounded-error estimation, and set inversion. DOI 10.1016/j.robot.2012.09.006. https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/abs/pii/S0921889012001522. S. Galerne is Simon Galerne, corroborated by the authors' subsequent publisher record https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/abs/pii/S0921889016307503. Used for set membership, not unknown association.

Neira and Tardós, 2001. Original paper confirms independent-gating failure, correlated innovations, joint compatibility, and branch-and-bound association. DOI 10.1109/70.976019. Author record https://webdiis.unizar.es/~neira/research.html. Original paper https://www.cs.cmu.edu/~motionplanning/papers/sbp_papers/n/00976019.pdf. Its probabilistic criterion is distinguished from deterministic interval feasibility.

Yang, Li, Campbell, and Jia, 2016. Author-hosted paper confirms globally optimal geometric registration by branch-and-bound under an ICP objective. DOI 10.1109/TPAMI.2015.2513405. https://jlyang.org/tpami16_go-icp_preprint.pdf. Used as an algorithmic precedent, not a bearing-only comparator.

Karney, 2013. Publisher record confirms ellipsoidal geodesic algorithms and metadata. DOI 10.1007/s00190-012-0578-z. https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s00190-012-0578-z. Derived bounds are distinguished from source claims about the numerical implementation.

Hall, 1935. Publisher record confirms representative theorem paper and metadata. DOI 10.1112/jlms/s1-10.37.26. https://londmathsoc.onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/abs/10.1112/jlms/s1-10.37.26.

Kuhn, 1955. Publisher abstract confirms assignment formulation and metadata. DOI 10.1002/nav.3800020109. https://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/10.1002/nav.3800020109. Used for finite assignment, not continuous localization optimality.
